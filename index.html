The Pillars of the Special Olympics

Understanding the Special Olympics rests on knowing its unique structure, the sports, and the mission of inclusion.

The World Games

The flagship event of the Special Olympics movement, held every two years and alternating between Summer and Winter Games.

  • Global Stage: Thousands of athletes from over 170 countries compete.
  • Unified Sports: Teams include athletes with and without intellectual disabilities.
  • A Celebration: More than just sports, it's a festival of inclusion and joy.

Athlete-Centered Approach

The focus is on personal bests, sportsmanship, and the joy of participation.

  • Skill-Based Divisions: Athletes compete against others of similar ability.
  • Athlete Leadership: Athletes are empowered to take on roles as leaders and spokespeople.
  • Healthy Athletes: Free health screenings are provided to all participants.

The Spirit of the Games

The Special Olympics is about more than just winning. It's about showcasing the human spirit.

Courage

Every athlete demonstrates immense courage by stepping onto the field of play to compete and try their best.

Joy

The pure, unfiltered joy of participation, achievement, and friendship is at the heart of every event.

Inclusion

The Games bring people from all backgrounds together, breaking down barriers and building a more inclusive world for all.
