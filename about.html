About the Special Olympics

We're here to celebrate abilities and foster inclusion through the power of sport.

Our Mission: The Inclusion Revolution

The Special Olympics mission is to provide year-round sports training and athletic competition in a variety of Olympic-type sports for children and adults with intellectual disabilities. This gives them continuing opportunities to develop physical fitness, demonstrate courage, experience joy, and participate in a sharing of gifts, skills, and friendship with their families, other Special Olympics athletes, and the community.

We believe in a world where every single person is accepted and welcomed, regardless of ability or disability. We are not just about sports; we are about building a global community of acceptance and action.

Our vision is to create a world of inclusion and community, where every single person is accepted and welcomed. Through the power of sports, we are fighting inactivity, injustice, and intolerance.

Our Guiding Principles

To be a truly transformative movement, we operate on a set of core values. This is our promise to the world.

Athlete-Centered Focus

Our athletes are at the heart of everything we do. We celebrate their abilities, their courage, and their personal achievements. We provide a platform for their voices to be heard.

Sportsmanship & Joy

We believe in the power of sports to teach valuable life lessons. We emphasize fair play, respect for others, and the pure joy of participation over winning at all costs.

Global Unity

The Special Olympics transcends borders, cultures, and languages. We unite people from all over the world in a shared celebration of the human spirit.

Inclusion & Acceptance

We are dedicated to creating a more inclusive world. Through Unified Sports and other initiatives, we bring people with and without intellectual disabilities together to play, learn, and grow.

Our Programs

We offer a wide range of programs beyond the World Games.

Sports & Competitions

Year-round training and competitions at local, national, and regional levels in over 20 different sports.

Health & Wellness

The Healthy Athletes program provides free health screenings and education to athletes, addressing critical health needs.

Join the Movement

The Special Olympics is more than an event; it's a movement for change. We invite you to explore our sports, read our athlete stories, and become part of our global community.
